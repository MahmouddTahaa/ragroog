# Sample Adult Hypertension — DEMO TEXT (not clinical advice)

This file is a **synthetic** guideline-shaped document for ingest tests. It is not a real protocol.

## Scope

This sample covers adult blood-pressure measurement and first-line treatment talk-tracks for a retrieval demo.

## Diagnosis

Confirm hypertension with repeated seated measurements. Use an appropriately sized cuff. Record the arm used and the device type.

## Non-drug measures

Advise sodium reduction, physical activity, and tobacco cessation. Reassess in four to twelve weeks when pressure is only mildly elevated and there is no organ damage.

## Drug treatment

When medication is indicated, start one agent and titrate. ACE inhibitors or ARBs, calcium-channel blockers, and thiazide-like diuretics are common first-line classes in many adult pathways.

## Follow-up

Recheck pressure after initiation or dose change. Ask about cough, dizziness, and adherence. Escalate if readings remain above target despite two agents.

## Referral

Refer urgently for suspected hypertensive emergency, pregnancy, or acute organ injury. This sample does not replace specialist judgement.
